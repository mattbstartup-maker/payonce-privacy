Privacy Policy — PayOnce PDF Scanner

Effective date: November 10, 2026
App: PayOnce PDF Scanner (Android package: com.mattstartup.scanonce)
Developer: Matt Startup

The short version

PayOnce PDF Scanner works entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission. Your documents, scans, and text are never uploaded anywhere by the app, and we never collect, sell, or share your personal data — because the app never receives it.

What the app stores, and where

Documents you scan or import, the pages and PDFs you create, and text recognized from your documents (OCR) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. You control this content: you can rename, export, share, or delete it at any time from inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its locally stored documents. If you choose to share or export a file (for example, sending a PDF by email), that sharing is done by you, through the app or service you pick, and is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Camera and document scanning

When you choose to scan, the app uses the document scanner provided by Google Play services (Google ML Kit). The camera is used only at that moment, at your direction. Image processing and text recognition (OCR) are performed on your device. Google Play services may download and update the machine-learning models it needs in the background; that is a function of Google Play services on your device, not of this app, and your document content is not sent to us.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking components, and it does not request internet access, so it has no way to transmit your information to us or to anyone else.

Data we share

None. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any personal data with third parties. The only exception would be disclosure required by law, and because we hold no user data, there would be nothing to disclose.

Purchases

PayOnce PDF Scanner is sold through Google Play. Your purchase is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
